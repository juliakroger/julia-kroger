# Blog editorial roadmap

This roadmap keeps the publication chronology intentional. Articles use their original cohort date in `date` and the latest substantial revision in `updated`.

## Editorial rules

- Write for anyone curious enough to ask, not specifically for juniors.
- Use direct, sassy English, real-world examples, and the flow: current situation → problem → what it actually needs.
- Keep overlapping concepts separate. Link between articles instead of repeating half an article inside another.
- Preserve the historical context of the publication date and label later facts in a short time-check section.
- Use current official or primary sources for technical and time-sensitive claims.
- Prefer original whiteboard-style illustrations. Do not copy another creator’s diagrams or branding.
- Do not put current market prices, TVL, yields, or other fast-expiring numbers into a historical article without an explicit as-of date.
- Use `featured: false` by default. Feature only the article currently chosen as the main entry point.
